The Big Question: What are tubes?


Vacuum tubes are devices that control current flow between two electrodes in the presence of a potential difference. They are housed in a glass bulb containing a vacuum, which is how they get their name. In guitar electronics, vacuum tubes are most commonly used in pre-amp boost stages, phase inverters, and power amp stages.


Though vacuum tubes were invented in the early 20th century, their popularity in the guitar world surged in 1948 when Leo Fender introduced the first Fender Champ amplifier, alongside the iconic Telecaster (then called the Broadcaster).
