I wanted to build something compact, like a guitar pedal, but with the warmth and characteristics of a tube amp. Inspired by David Gilmour's use of the BK Butler Tube Driver, which achieves a similar effect, I set out to create my own design.


The Tube Driver uses a transistor stage to boost the signal, which is then fed into a 12AX7 pre-amp stage to achieve that classic tube tone. 


I decided to structure my design into four main components: an Op-amp gain stage, a 12AX7 tube gain stage, tone controls, and the power supply.
