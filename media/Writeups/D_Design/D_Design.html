I began by building the power supply. The 12AX7 tube heaters require 12V at 300mA AC, while the grid and Op-amp need DC power, so I needed a rectifier and filter setup.


In my initial design, I used a traditional rectifier circuit along with two 470µF filter capacitors. However, when I plotted the output voltage, it wasn’t fully stable.


To address this, I improved the design by adding extra capacitors, which helped smooth out the voltage and provide a more consistent output.
