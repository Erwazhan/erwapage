The Fender-Marshall-Vox (FMV) tone stack is a circuit that shapes the output frequencies of an audio signal. Originally designed by Leo Fender for the Bassman 5F6-A amplifier, it was later adopted by Marshall and Vox in their iconic designs.


At its core, the FMV tone stack functions as a passive network of low and high-pass filters. A key feature of this design is that the overall frequency response is interdependent on the settings of all the tone control potentiometers. Due to this nonlinear behavior, I have developed an interactive graph that allows me to input various values and fine-tune the circuit to achieve my desired frequency response.
